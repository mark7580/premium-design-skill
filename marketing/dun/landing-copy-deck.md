## How it works (3 steps)

Frame as **Need → Decision → Action**, not as marketplace mechanics.

1. **Ask.** Type the question the way you'd ask a friend. What something
   costs, who can do it, whether it's worth fixing.
2. **Decide.** DUN turns your question into real numbers, real availability,
   and real people — compared side by side, in plain language.
3. **It gets done.** Book it, schedule it, track it. One conversation from
   first question to finished work.
